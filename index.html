3rd St Albans

Weather Station

This is the public page for the 3rd St Albans Scouts weather station at the Scout Hut. It shows current conditions and recent history so families, leaders and the community can see what the weather’s doing.

Cookies & privacy

We don’t use advertising cookies. To run the site we may store a small preference in your browser (so this message only shows once) and use anonymous page-view counts via GoatCounter.

Tap I understand to continue. Full details are always available from the Privacy button.

Privacy

How we use this site

Who we are. This page is run for 3rd St Albans Scouts as a public weather display for the Scout Hut.

What we show. Weather readings and charts. We do not ask for names, emails, or details about young people on this page.

Cookies & storage. We do not use advertising cookies. Your browser may keep a small “welcome / consent” note so the intro only appears once.

Analytics. After you accept the intro, we use GoatCounter for anonymous page-view counts (for example visits and rough country). It is not used to identify you personally.
